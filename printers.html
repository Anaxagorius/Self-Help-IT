    Printers & Scanners

    The classics — paper jams, missing printers, and scanner woes.

    🖨️ Printer isn't printing anything
    Most Common Easy Fix
    ▼

    Nine times out of ten, it's one of these simple things. Walk through them in order.

    1. 1 Look at the printer itself — is it powered on? Is the power light steady (not blinking red)?
    2. 2 Check the paper tray — is there paper loaded? Is it jammed? (See the paper jam guide below.)
    3. 3 On your computer, go to Start → Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners. Find your printer and click "Open print queue". Delete any stuck jobs (right-click → Cancel).
    4. 4 Right-click the printer in the Printers list → "Set as default printer" if it isn't already.
    5. 5 Restart the Print Spooler service: Press Windows key, type Services, open it, scroll to Print Spooler, right-click → Restart.
    6. 6 Try printing a test page: right-click the printer → "Printer properties" → "Print Test Page".
    📄 Paper jam
    Easy Fix
    ▼

    Paper jams are annoying but rarely serious. Take your time — rushing can tear the paper and make it worse.

    1. 1 Turn off the printer first. This releases the rollers and makes it easier to remove the paper safely.
    2. 2 Gently open all access panels (front, back, and the paper tray). Look inside with a flashlight if needed.
    3. 3 Carefully pull the jammed paper in the direction it feeds (usually forward, in the direction the paper would have come out). Pull slowly and evenly — don't yank!
    4. 4 Remove all pieces — even tiny bits. A scrap left inside will cause more jams.
    5. 5 Close all panels, power the printer back on, and try printing again.
    📞 If you can't locate or safely remove the paper, or the printer shows a jam error with no visible paper, stop and call IT. Forcing it could damage the rollers.
    🔍 Can't find the printer in the list
    Moderate
    ▼

    This often happens after a computer update or when using a different machine.

    1. 1 Go to Start → Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → Add a printer or scanner. Wait for Windows to scan the network.
    2. 2 If the printer doesn't appear, click "The printer that I want isn't listed" and choose "Find a printer by name". Enter the printer name (ask a colleague or check the label on the printer).
    3. 3 Make sure you're connected to the office network (not guest Wi-Fi) — printers are only accessible on the internal network.
    📞 If you can't add the printer after these steps, IT will need to push the printer to your computer remotely. Give us a call!
    📠 Scanner not scanning / not recognized
    Moderate
    ▼

    Most of our scanners are part of the multifunction printers. Here's how to get them scanning again.

    1. 1 Ensure the document is properly placed on the glass or in the document feeder. Make sure nothing is sticking out.
    2. 2 Open Windows Fax and Scan (search in Start menu) and try scanning from there.
    3. 3 Power-cycle the printer/scanner: turn it off, wait 30 seconds, turn it back on.
    4. 4 If using the scan-to-email feature, make sure the email address on file is your current work email. Check with IT if you've recently changed accounts.

    Stop Troubleshooting — Call IT Right Now

    Some situations go beyond self-help. If any of the following apply to you, put down the mouse and give us a call straight away. At the credit union, protecting member data is our top priority.

    🦠 You see pop-ups demanding payment, or your files appear to be encrypted with a ransom note.
    🔓 You suspect your account has been hacked or someone else is accessing your computer remotely.
    📧 You clicked a link or opened an attachment in a suspicious email and now something feels wrong.
    🏦 The core banking system is completely down and members are waiting at the counter.
    💾 You accidentally deleted important member or financial data that cannot be recovered locally.
    🔥 Your computer is making unusual noises, smells like burning, or is physically damaged.
    🔒 Your account is fully locked out and you cannot access anything — email, Windows, or banking.
    📞 Someone calls claiming to be "IT support" asking for your password or remote access. Hang up. Call us to verify.
    ⚠️ You see an unfamiliar error message on a banking screen that you've never seen before and don't understand.
    🖥️ Your computer gets a Blue Screen repeatedly — more than once in a short period.
    👤 You notice a new user account or program installed on your computer that you didn't create.
    🌐 The entire branch has lost network/internet access and there's no known outage listed above.
