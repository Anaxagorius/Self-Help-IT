    Software & Apps

    Self-help guides for Fiserv Financial-net, Valley CU Mobile App, Finicity, ASAPP, Collabria, and Desjardins Employer Solutions.

    🏦 Fiserv Financial-net – Can't log in
    Common Moderate
    ▼

    Fiserv Financial-net is our core banking platform. Login issues are usually quick to resolve.

    1. 1 Confirm Caps Lock is off and you are using your Financial-net credentials — these are separate from your Windows or Microsoft 365 password.
    2. 2 Make sure you are on the credit union network or connected through VPN if working remotely. Financial-net is not accessible from open internet without VPN.
    3. 3 Clear your browser cache (Ctrl + Shift + Delete → select "All time" → clear cached images and files) and try logging in again.
    4. 4 If you see a "session expired" or "already logged in" message, close all browser windows completely and reopen them, then try again.
    5. 5 If your account appears locked after failed attempts, do not keep trying — this can trigger a longer lockout. Contact IT to reset it.
    📞 If you cannot log in after these steps, or your account is locked, call IT right away — teller access must not stay down.
    ⏳ Fiserv Financial-net – Running slowly or freezing
    Moderate
    ▼

    Sluggish performance in Financial-net can affect member service. Try these steps first.

    1. 1 Check with other staff — if multiple people are affected, it is likely a server or network issue. Contact IT to investigate.
    2. 2 Close any extra browser tabs and unused programs to free up memory on your computer.
    3. 3 Log out and back in to Financial-net to refresh your session.
    4. 4 Try switching to a different supported browser (e.g., Microsoft Edge if you're using Chrome, or vice versa).
    5. 5 If the issue persists, restart your computer and try again before calling IT.
    📞 Persistent slowness affecting member service? Call IT — we can check server and network health remotely.
    📱 Valley CU Mobile App – Member can't log in or use the app
    Common Easy Fix
    ▼

    The Valley CU Mobile Banking App is powered by the Fiserv platform. Here's how to guide members through common issues.

    1. 1 Ask the member to confirm they are using the correct username and password for online/mobile banking — this is different from branch or phone PINs.
    2. 2 Check if the app is up to date. Ask the member to open the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android), search for "Valley Credit Union", and install any pending updates.
    3. 3 If the member is getting a "too many login attempts" message, their account may be temporarily locked. Have them wait 15 minutes and try again, or direct them to call Member Services.
    4. 4 Ask the member to close the app completely (swipe it away) and reopen it.
    5. 5 If the app shows an error message, note the exact wording and error code (if any) and report it to IT — this helps us identify platform-wide issues quickly.
    6. 6 As a last resort, guide the member to delete and reinstall the app. Their account data is stored on the Fiserv servers, so reinstalling does not delete their information.
    ℹ️ The Valley CU Mobile App is available on the App Store and Google Play. More info at valleycreditunion.com.
    💳 Finicity by Mastercard – Account linking issues
    Moderate May Need IT
    ▼

    Finicity by Mastercard provides financial data aggregation and open banking services. Members use it to link external accounts for verification or budgeting features.

    1. 1 If a member reports that an external account won't link, first confirm they are entering the online banking credentials for that institution — not their Valley CU credentials.
    2. 2 Ask if they recently changed their password at the external bank — the Finicity connection will break if credentials change and must be re-linked.
    3. 3 Some institutions require the member to complete a multi-factor authentication (MFA) step within the linking flow. Ask the member to check for an SMS code or authenticator prompt.
    4. 4 If the connection was working before but suddenly broke, the Finicity service or the external bank's API may be temporarily unavailable. Ask the member to try again later or the next business day.
    5. 5 Note the name of the institution the member is trying to link and any error message shown. Escalate to IT if the problem is repeating for multiple members or multiple accounts.
    📞 If Finicity linking is failing for multiple members or you suspect a service outage, contact IT so we can check the integration status.
    🤝 ASAPP (via League Data) – Platform or feature not working
    Moderate May Need IT
    ▼

    ASAPP is our Open Experience Platform (OXP) delivered through League Data, providing digital banking features to Atlantic Canadian credit unions including Valley CU.

    1. 1 If a specific feature or screen is not loading, try refreshing the page or restarting the app. Some features load asynchronously and a refresh often resolves it.
    2. 2 Check whether the issue affects all users or just one member. Widespread issues are likely platform-level and should be reported to IT immediately so we can contact League Data support.
    3. 3 If a member reports that their data is not showing correctly (wrong balances, missing transactions), advise them to log out and back in. If the issue persists, log the member's name and account info and escalate to IT.
    4. 4 Note the exact error message or behavior the member or staff member is experiencing — screenshots are very helpful. Pass this to IT so we can relay it to League Data / ASAPP support accurately.
    5. 5 Do not advise members to attempt workarounds involving their account settings or profile in the ASAPP platform without IT guidance, as some changes can affect their access.
    ℹ️ ASAPP powers digital banking features across 39 Atlantic Canadian credit unions via League Data's OXP partnership. More info at asappbanking.com.
    📞 ASAPP issues often require escalation to League Data. Contact IT and we will raise a support ticket with League Data on your behalf.
    💳 Collabria – Credit card system issues
    Common Moderate
    ▼

    Collabria provides credit card services for Valley Credit Union members. Here's how to handle common issues.

    1. 1 For member inquiries about their Collabria card (balance, transactions, disputes), direct them to the Collabria Cardholder Services number on the back of their card or the Collabria website. Valley CU staff do not have direct access to Collabria cardholder account details.
    2. 2 If you are trying to access the Collabria staff portal and cannot log in, check that you are using your Collabria-specific staff credentials (separate from your Valley CU Windows login).
    3. 3 If the Collabria portal is loading slowly or not responding, first check if other staff are experiencing the same issue. If yes, it may be a Collabria service outage — note the time and contact IT.
    4. 4 For new card applications or limit increases that are not processing, verify all required fields are complete and that the member's information in our system matches what Collabria has on file. Discrepancies can cause processing errors.
    5. 5 If a member's card is declined at point of sale, this is handled by Collabria directly. Direct the member to call the number on the back of their card. If the issue appears systemic (multiple members), contact IT.
    ℹ️ Collabria credit card services for Valley Credit Union members: collabriacreditcards.ca.
    📞 For Collabria portal outages or issues affecting multiple members, contact IT and we will reach out to Collabria support directly.
    🏢 Desjardins Employer Solutions – Payroll or benefits access issues
    Moderate May Need IT
    ▼

    Desjardins Employer Solutions handles our employee group benefits and related HR services. Here's what to try if you have trouble accessing the platform.

    1. 1 Confirm you are visiting the correct Desjardins Employer Solutions portal URL. Bookmark it once you have the right one to avoid landing on an incorrect page.
    2. 2 Your Desjardins credentials are separate from your Valley CU Windows login. If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the Desjardins login page to reset it via your registered email.
    3. 3 If you are not receiving the MFA verification code, check your spam/junk folder. Also confirm that the email address registered with Desjardins is your current Valley CU email address.
    4. 4 If you see a "permission denied" or "access restricted" message, your account role may not have access to the section you need. Contact your manager or HR to verify your access level.
    5. 5 For benefit claims or payroll discrepancies, these are handled by HR, not IT. Please reach out to HR directly. IT can only assist with technical access issues.
    6. 6 If the Desjardins portal is completely unreachable or showing a server error, it may be a service outage on Desjardins' end. Check back in 30 minutes or contact IT so we can investigate further.
    📞 If you remain locked out of Desjardins Employer Solutions after these steps, contact IT. We will work with Desjardins support to restore your access.

    Stop Troubleshooting — Call IT Right Now

    Some situations go beyond self-help. If any of the following apply to you, put down the mouse and give us a call straight away. At the credit union, protecting member data is our top priority.

    🦠 You see pop-ups demanding payment, or your files appear to be encrypted with a ransom note.
    🔓 You suspect your account has been hacked or someone else is accessing your computer remotely.
    📧 You clicked a link or opened an attachment in a suspicious email and now something feels wrong.
    🏦 The core banking system is completely down and members are waiting at the counter.
    💾 You accidentally deleted important member or financial data that cannot be recovered locally.
    🔥 Your computer is making unusual noises, smells like burning, or is physically damaged.
    🔒 Your account is fully locked out and you cannot access anything — email, Windows, or banking.
    📞 Someone calls claiming to be "IT support" asking for your password or remote access. Hang up. Call us to verify.
    ⚠️ You see an unfamiliar error message on a banking or software screen that you've never seen before and don't understand.
    🖥️ Your computer gets a Blue Screen repeatedly — more than once in a short period.
    👤 You notice a new user account or program installed on your computer that you didn't create.
    🌐 The entire branch has lost network/internet access and there's no known outage listed above.
