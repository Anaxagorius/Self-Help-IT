    Mambu

    Most probable incidents & practical employee guidance based on Mambu documentation and real‑world feedback.

    🔒 Can't log in to Mambu
    Most Common Easy Fix
    ▼

    Endless loading screen, sudden logout after an update, or a blank page? This fix resolves about 90% of Mambu login issues.

    1. 1 Log out of Mambu completely if you're stuck on a loading screen.
    2. 2 Close the browser entirely — don't just close the tab.
    3. 3 Clear your browser cache & cookies — press Ctrl + Shift + Delete, select "All time", tick Cookies and Cached images, then click Clear.
    4. 4 Reopen the browser and log back in to Mambu.
    5. 5 If access is still blocked, confirm that your role still includes the required permissions — an admin change may have affected your account.
    💡 Tip: If many staff are affected at once, check the Mambu Status Page or the internal IT Teams channel before escalating.
    📞 If your access is still blocked after clearing cache, contact your internal admin — not Mambu Support directly. For account lockouts after failed login attempts, let IT know.
    ⏳ Mambu is running slowly or appears frozen
    Moderate
    ▼

    Pages loading slowly, search taking forever, or transactions appearing stuck? This is usually a planned maintenance window or a regional cloud performance event — not a crash.

    1. 1 Do not retry transactions repeatedly — submitting multiple times during slowness can cause duplicate actions once the system recovers.
    2. 2 Avoid refreshing transaction pages while a submission is in progress.
    3. 3 Check the internal Teams IT channel or the Mambu Status Page for any posted maintenance notice.
    4. 4 If no notice is posted and slowness persists beyond 10 minutes, let IT know so we can check network and server health.
    5. 5 Resume normal work only after IT or the status page confirms service is healthy.
    💡 Important: Retrying during a slowdown can cause duplicate transactions once the system recovers. When in doubt, wait and verify.
    ❌ Transaction failed or "cannot be completed"
    Moderate May Need IT
    ▼

    Deposit or disbursement won't approve, or an error appears with no clear reason? Most transaction failures are business-rule validations enforced by Mambu — not system faults.

    1. 1 Check the account status — the account must be active and approved. Closed or pending accounts cannot process transactions.
    2. 2 Confirm that all required approval steps have been completed before attempting the transaction.
    3. 3 Verify the amount and dates are within the product's configured limits.
    4. 4 Ensure your user account has transaction rights for this action — permissions may have changed after an admin update.
    5. 5 If the transaction is still partially posted or stuck, do not retry — note the timestamp and account ID and escalate.
    📞 If you can't identify the cause, capture the timestamp, account ID, and the exact error message, then escalate internally with that evidence. Do not retry a failed transaction before checking with IT.
    🔍 Can't find a member or account in Mambu
    Easy Fix
    ▼

    A member exists in the system but search returns nothing? Mambu's search prioritises exact and categorised matches — it's not a fuzzy Google-style search.

    1. 1 Remove all active filters before searching — a leftover filter is the most common reason nothing appears.
    2. 2 Search using the full name or member/account ID rather than a partial name.
    3. 3 Confirm you are in the correct search category — searching under "Clients" won't return account numbers, and vice versa.
    4. 4 If a record was very recently created, allow a minute or two — there can be a brief search indexing delay before new records appear.
    💡 Always start with the member ID or account number when available — name searches can miss records if spelling is slightly off.
    🌐 Mambu appears to be down or unavailable
    Moderate
    ▼

    Mambu is a cloud SaaS platform — scheduled maintenance and brief regional interruptions are a normal part of operations, not signs of a failing system.

    1. 1 Check the internal IT Teams channel or the banner at the top of this portal — a notice is posted when we're aware of an issue.
    2. 2 Verify on the Mambu Status Page whether a maintenance event or incident has been declared.
    3. 3 Pause any sensitive operations — don't attempt to process transactions or approvals during a declared outage.
    4. 4 Resume work only once IT or the status page confirms the platform is fully operational.
    📞 If no outage is shown on the status page but Mambu is unreachable for your whole branch, call IT — it may be a local network issue rather than Mambu itself.
    🚫 Permissions missing for tasks I used to do
    May Need IT
    ▼

    After a system update or admin change you may find that actions or menu items that were available before are now missing or greyed out.

    1. 1 First, log out and back in — your session may be holding a cached version of your old permissions.
    2. 2 Clear your browser cache (Ctrl + Shift + Delete) and log in again to ensure the latest role settings are loaded.
    3. 3 Confirm with a colleague whether they have the same permission — if it's missing for everyone, it may be a deliberate admin change.
    4. 4 If the permission is missing only for you, contact your internal Mambu admin to review and restore your role assignment.
    💡 Mambu permission changes are made by your internal admin team — Mambu Support cannot change your organisation's role settings directly.
    📚 "Mambu is confusing — I'm not sure I'm doing this right"
    Guidance
    ▼

    Mambu is a powerful platform that requires some learning. Many reported "incidents" turn out to be process questions — not technical failures. You're not alone.

    1. 1 Check whether your question is covered in the internal how-to guides shared by your team lead — these are written in plain language for your specific role, unlike the official Mambu manuals.
    2. 2 Ask a colleague who regularly uses that feature — a quick 2-minute walkthrough is often faster than any documentation.
    3. 3 If you're unsure whether you've completed a step correctly, stop before submitting and double-check with your supervisor rather than guessing.
    4. 4 Request a training refresher through your team lead if a particular workflow consistently causes confusion — this protects both you and members.
    💡 Good to know: Mambu's official documentation is written for technical administrators, not frontline staff. If something in the docs feels overly complex, that's normal — ask internally for a plain-language explanation.

    Stop Troubleshooting — Call IT Right Now

    Some situations go beyond self-help. If any of the following apply to you, put down the mouse and give us a call straight away. At the credit union, protecting member data is our top priority.

    🦠 You see pop-ups demanding payment, or your files appear to be encrypted with a ransom note.
    🔓 You suspect your account has been hacked or someone else is accessing your computer remotely.
    📧 You clicked a link or opened an attachment in a suspicious email and now something feels wrong.
    🏦 The core banking system is completely down and members are waiting at the counter.
    💾 You accidentally deleted important member or financial data that cannot be recovered locally.
    🔥 Your computer is making unusual noises, smells like burning, or is physically damaged.
    🔒 Your account is fully locked out and you cannot access anything — email, Windows, or banking.
    📞 Someone calls claiming to be "IT support" asking for your password or remote access. Hang up. Call us to verify.
    ⚠️ A Mambu transaction partially posted and you're unsure if it completed — do not retry. Call IT with the timestamp and account ID.
    🖥️ Your computer gets a Blue Screen repeatedly — more than once in a short period.
    👤 You notice a new user account or program installed on your computer that you didn't create.
    🌐 The entire branch has lost network/internet access and there's no known outage listed above.
